VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of January 1, 2023 ("Effective Date") between Acme Corp ("Vendor") and ClientCo LLC ("Client").

1. Services. Vendor shall provide software development services as described in Exhibit A. Client shall pay Vendor [PAYMENT AMOUNT NOT SPECIFIED] within 30 days of invoice.

2. Intellectual Property. All work product created under this Agreement shall be owned by Vendor, subject to a perpetual license to Client.

3. Confidentiality. Each party agrees to maintain confidentiality of the other party's proprietary information for a period of 2 years following termination.

4. Limitation of Liability. In no event shall either party be liable for indirect damages.

5. IP Assignment. Client shall own all deliverables produced under this Agreement. This contradicts Section 2.
